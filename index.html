PERCEPTION TRIALS — ROOM 1

The Optic Bench

Seven stations, three ways to play. Watch it like a show, race a friend, or train your own eye.

Full Game

One endless run. Random station, ramps through six difficulty stages, then jumps to a new station. Built for groups.

→

Individual Station

Pick one of the seven trials and run it on its own, ramping through difficulty on a loop.

→

Eye Test

No timer, no crowd. Pick a station and difficulty, click your answer, see if you were right.

→

Settings

ANSWER TIME PER ROUND

Applies to Full Game and Individual Station. Eye Test is always untimed.

SESSION LENGTH

PACE BETWEEN ROUNDS

Controls the transition and reveal time between rounds — never your answer timer.

PLAY STYLE

Solo: click your answer(s) each round. Friends: no input — the round just plays out and reveals the answer.

NARRATOR VOICE

Your browser's built-in text-to-speech. It has opinions, and knows when to shut up.
